# Product Experimentation

Use controlled experiments when uncertainty is material, traffic is sufficient, the treatment can be isolated, and a measurable outcome can resolve the decision.

For smaller products, use instrumented staged rollouts, before/after comparisons, usability sessions, or direct observation when they produce better evidence than underpowered A/B tests.

Define hypothesis, population, primary metric, guardrails, exposure assignment, duration/stop rule, and interpretation before launch. Preserve experiment assignment consistently across the relevant journey.
