# Hierarchy and Progressive Disclosure

Expose the information and actions needed for the current decision, while keeping advanced controls reachable.

Use hierarchy through typography, grouping, spacing, placement and order before adding decoration.

Progressive disclosure fits:
- advanced configuration;
- infrequent destructive or administrative actions;
- secondary metadata;
- expert controls that would overwhelm the primary workflow.

Keep controls visible when they are frequent, required to understand current state, or costly to discover late.

Review whether users must open multiple surfaces simply to understand the current state. Disclosure should reduce cognitive load without hiding essential context.
