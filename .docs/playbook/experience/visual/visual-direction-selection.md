# Visual Direction Selection

Choose a visual direction from product character and usage conditions, then express it through a small consistent system.

## Utility / productivity
Compact spacing, clear typography, restrained surfaces and color. Fits frequent task-oriented tools and data-heavy products.

## Minimal / editorial
Strong typography, generous whitespace, limited chrome and deliberate imagery. Fits products where content, focus or perceived refinement matters.

## Warm / approachable
Softer neutrals, moderate radii, human copy and restrained expressive color. Fits consumer, education and relationship-oriented products.

## Technical / operational
Dense layout, strong status hierarchy, precise alignment, monospace accents where meaningful and high information visibility. Fits developer, monitoring and operational tools.

## Expressive consumer
Stronger brand color, imagery, motion and shape language. Fits products where discovery, identity or emotion materially contributes to value.

After choosing, define typography, spacing, surface levels, radii, border/elevation rules, color roles, iconography, motion and density. Use a representative screen to validate the system before styling every route independently.
