# Terms of Service

A Terms of Service (ToS) agreement defines the legal relationship between you and your users. It sets out what users can and cannot do with the product, what you are responsible for, and how disputes are resolved. A missing or incomplete ToS leaves you without recourse when users misuse the product or dispute a billing action.

## Key Clauses

### Acceptable Use

Define what users may and may not do with the product. Be specific about prohibited conduct:
- Attempting to reverse engineer, scrape, or otherwise extract data beyond normal use
- Sending spam, malware, or illegal content through the product
- Sharing account access in violation of the subscription terms
- Using the product for illegal purposes

An acceptable use policy is also the basis for account termination — without it, you have no contractual ground to close an account engaging in abusive behavior.

### Account Termination

Define the conditions under which you may suspend or terminate an account:
- Violation of the acceptable use policy
- Non-payment after a grace period
- Account compromise or suspected fraudulent activity
- Closure of the service

State whether you provide notice before termination (and what notice period) or whether certain violations (e.g., illegal activity) result in immediate termination.

### Liability Limitations

Limit your liability to the extent allowed by applicable law. Standard clauses:
- Service is provided "as is" without warranty of uninterrupted availability
- Your liability for damages is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months preceding the claim
- You are not liable for indirect, consequential, or incidental damages

Consult a lawyer to ensure limitations are enforceable in your jurisdiction and target markets.

### Dispute Resolution

Specify how disputes are handled:
- **Governing law:** Which state/country's law governs the agreement
- **Jurisdiction:** Which courts have jurisdiction if litigation occurs
- **Arbitration clause:** Many SaaS companies require binding arbitration for individual disputes and exclude class actions

### ToS Changes

You will update your ToS. State how users will be notified:

```
We may update these Terms from time to time. For material changes, we will notify users 
via email or a prominent in-app notice at least 30 days before the changes take effect. 
Continued use of the service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the 
updated Terms. If you do not agree to the updated Terms, you may close your account 
before the effective date.
```

**Notification for material changes is not optional under GDPR** for terms that affect data processing. Silently updating terms does not bind users who were not notified.

## What ToS Cannot Do

A ToS cannot:
- Override statutory consumer protection rights (e.g., GDPR data subject rights)
- Waive liability for fraud or willful misconduct
- Impose unreasonable restrictions that courts will strike as unconscionable

Courts routinely strike ToS clauses that are one-sided to the point of being unfair. Clarity and proportionality serve you better than maximally aggressive limitation clauses.
