# Landing Page Copy

Landing page copy converts visitors into users. It works section by section, answering the visitor's implicit questions in sequence: "Is this for me?" → "Do I trust them?" → "Do I understand what it does?" → "Is it worth it?" → "What do I do next?"

## Section-by-Section Copy Guidance

### Navigation

The nav CTA label should match the hero CTA or be a natural step before it. If the hero CTA is "Start free trial," the nav CTA should be "Try free" or "Get started."

### Hero

See [design/content/hero-copy.md](../../experience/content/hero-copy.md) for headline formulas.

**Headline:** One of three formulas — outcome-focused, problem-focused, or audience-focused. Specific. Benefit-driven. No jargon before the user knows the product.

**Subheadline:** Expands the headline with what the product is and who it's for. 1–2 sentences.

**CTA:** Verb + specific outcome. "Start your 14-day trial" beats "Get started." If it's free, say so: "Try free for 14 days — no credit card required."

### Social Proof / Logos

The copy here is minimal — the logos do the work:
- "Trusted by teams at [Company A], [Company B], and [Company C]"
- Or just logos with no copy (stronger if the logos are recognizable)

If you don't have logos, use a user count: "Joined by 1,200 freelancers" (only use real numbers).

### Problem Statement

Write from inside the user's experience, not from the outside:

**Outside (weak):** "Many freelancers struggle with getting paid on time."

**Inside (strong):** "You send the invoice. Then you wait. Then you send a polite follow-up. Then another one. Then you feel awkward. Then the payment finally arrives — 47 days late."

Put the visitor in the problem. The more specifically you describe their experience, the more they nod and keep reading.

### Demo / Solution Section

Show the product solving the problem described above. Options:
- **Screenshot with callouts:** "Here's where you set up automatic reminders"
- **Short product video (60–90 seconds):** Demonstrates the core workflow
- **Animated GIF:** Shows the key interaction in context

Write minimal copy here — the visual does the work. A one-sentence caption per callout is enough.

### Feature-to-Benefit Translation

Every feature listed must be translated into a user benefit:

| Feature (what it is) | Benefit (what it does for the user) |
|---|---|
| Automatic payment reminders | You get paid without sending awkward follow-up emails |
| Custom invoice templates | Clients see a polished invoice that reflects your brand |
| Stripe integration | Clients pay online in one click — no bank transfer friction |

Never list feature names without the associated benefit. "Stripe integration" tells the visitor what exists; "clients pay online in one click" tells them why they care.

### Testimonials

Select testimonials that reflect the specific benefit or differentiation in the positioning statement — not generic praise:

**Generic (weak):** "Great tool! Really love it!" — @randomuser

**Specific (strong):** "I used to spend two hours a week chasing invoices. Since switching to Invoice Pilot, I get paid in under a week and I've never sent a follow-up." — @sarahdesigner, Freelance Brand Designer

Selection criteria:
- Names the specific problem that was solved
- Includes a measurable outcome if possible
- Comes from a recognizable member of your target audience

### FAQ Section

See [design/layouts/pricing.md](../../experience/layouts/pricing.md) for FAQ guidance. Address the top 5 objections to signing up.

### Final CTA

Repeat the primary CTA from the hero. Add a secondary reassurance line below: "No credit card required. Cancel anytime."
