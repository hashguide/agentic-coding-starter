# Marketing Overview

Marketing for a solo builder is a resource allocation problem. You have limited time and budget, and most marketing channels require significant investment before they return results. The goal is to pick 1–2 channels that match your audience and compound, rather than spread thinly across everything.

## Channel Comparison

| Channel | Effort | Cost | Time to results | Compounds? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Content / SEO** | High initial (writing, optimization) | Low ($0 + hosting) | 3–12 months | Yes — content attracts indefinitely |
| **Paid acquisition** | Medium (setup, monitoring, iteration) | High (ad spend) | Immediate | No — stops when spend stops |
| **Organic social** | Medium-High (consistent creation) | Low | 1–6 months | Partially — audience is platform-dependent |
| **Communities** | Low-Medium (genuine participation) | Low | Weeks | Partially — relationships compound |
| **Partnerships** | Medium (finding + negotiating) | Low-Medium | 1–3 months | Yes — distribution multiplier |

## Recommended Sequencing for a Solo Builder

**Phase 1 — Pre-launch (building an audience before you ship):**
- Build in public on one social platform (X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Bluesky — wherever your target user is)
- Collect emails: landing page with a waitlist form before the product exists
- Participate in 2–3 relevant communities without pitching the product — establish presence first

**Phase 2 — Launch:**
- Activate the waitlist
- Submit to Product Hunt, relevant subreddits, Hacker News (Show HN), and niche newsletters
- Ask your personal network to spread the word — the initial burst matters for algorithm signals

**Phase 3 — Post-launch growth:**
- Start one content/SEO channel if you have patience for 3–12 month payoffs — comparison pages and tutorials often rank faster than general blog posts
- Continue community presence; move from participant to occasional problem-solver with links to your product
- Evaluate paid acquisition only after you have a validated, profitable unit economics story (know your LTV before setting a CAC target)

**What to skip early:**
- Posting to every social platform simultaneously — produces mediocre content everywhere
- Press outreach — works well for funding announcements, poorly for cold product pitches
- Influencer marketing — high cost and low ROI at early-stage volumes
